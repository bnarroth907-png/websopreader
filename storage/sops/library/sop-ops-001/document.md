# Equipment Maintenance Procedure

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-001  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-15  
**Owner:** Operations Team

## Purpose
To ensure all equipment is maintained in optimal condition to prevent failures and ensure safety.

## Scope
Applies to all production and support equipment in the facility.

## Responsibilities
- **Technicians:** Perform maintenance tasks
- **Supervisors:** Verify completion and quality
- **Safety Officer:** Ensure compliance with safety standards

## Procedure

### 1. Pre-Maintenance Checks
1. Review equipment history and previous maintenance logs
2. Gather required tools and spare parts
3. Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) the equipment
4. Verify energy isolation

### 2. Maintenance Steps
1. Clean equipment surfaces
2. Inspect for wear and damage
3. Lubricate moving parts according to manufacturer specs
4. Replace worn components
5. Calibrate sensors and controls

### 3. Post-Maintenance
1. Remove LOTO devices
2. Test equipment operation
3. Document findings and actions taken
4. Update maintenance schedule

## Safety Precautions
- Always follow LOTO procedures
- Wear appropriate PPE
- Report any anomalies immediately

## Records
Maintain maintenance logs for a minimum of 3 years.
