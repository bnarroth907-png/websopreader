# Customer Complaint Handling Procedure

**Document ID:** SOP-CS-003  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-10  
**Owner:** Customer Success Team

## Purpose
To ensure consistent, professional, and timely resolution of customer complaints.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Receive Complaint
1. Listen actively without interrupting
2. Record details: date, time, customer info, issue description
3. Acknowledge the concern empathetically

### Step 2: Investigate
1. Gather relevant information and records
2. Consult with involved departments
3. Determine root cause

### Step 3: Resolve
1. Propose solution to customer
2. Obtain customer agreement
3. Implement corrective action
4. Document resolution

### Step 4: Follow-up
1. Contact customer within 48 hours to confirm satisfaction
2. Update CRM system
3. Identify process improvements

## Escalation
Escalate to manager if:
- Resolution exceeds authority level
- Customer remains dissatisfied after two attempts
- Legal or safety implications exist

## Response Times
- Initial acknowledgment: within 4 business hours
- Resolution target: within 5 business days
