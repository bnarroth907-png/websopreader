# Workplace Safety Protocol

**Document ID:** SOP-SAF-002  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** 2025-11-01  
**Owner:** EHS Department

## Purpose
To establish minimum safety standards and prevent workplace incidents.

## General Rules
1. Report all incidents and near-misses immediately
2. Use designated walkways
3. Keep work areas clean and organized
4. Follow all posted safety signs

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Safety glasses in all production areas
- Hard hats in construction zones
- Hearing protection above 85 dB
- Steel-toed boots in warehouse

## Emergency Procedures
### Fire
1. Activate nearest fire alarm
2. Evacuate via nearest exit
3. Assemble at designated muster point
4. Do not use elevators

### Medical Emergency
1. Call emergency services (911)
2. Provide first aid if trained
3. Notify supervisor
4. Do not move injured person unless necessary

## Training
All employees must complete safety orientation within first week of employment.
